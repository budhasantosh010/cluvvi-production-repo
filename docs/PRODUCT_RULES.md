# Product rules

## The one V0 success question

Can Cluvvi produce 20 leads that we genuinely believe are worth contacting for the video-editing SaaS?

A feature that does not directly improve that result is not part of V0.

## Trust rules

- Never turn a weak profile match into a claim of active demand.
- Every final factual claim must cite a stored source document.
- Facts, inferences, limitations, and recommended actions are visibly separate.
- No guessed contact route is presented as verified.
- Scores remain explainable by dimension; do not hide judgment behind one opaque intent score.
- Unknown information stays unknown.
- Provider failure must be visible and must not corrupt state.

## Experience rules

The product should feel like one guided job, not a collection of tools:

1. Describe what you sell.
2. Confirm what Cluvvi understood.
3. Confirm where Cluvvi will look.
4. Start finding customers.
5. Review truthful progress.
6. Receive the ranked opportunities.

Each page has one obvious primary action. Advanced controls are secondary and explained in plain language. Progress reports completed work instead of invented percentages.

## Safety and scope

Use official APIs, permitted providers, and public business information. Do not bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or platform rules. Do not collect private messages, private browsing data, home addresses, sensitive categories, or unrelated personal data.
